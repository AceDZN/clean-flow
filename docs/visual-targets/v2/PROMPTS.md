## Notes for the builder

- **Art direction that worked:** a *floating* sandstone island on a flat peach-to-coral gradient background (no horizon, no surrounding terrain) gives the "whole level fits the screen" look. Camera ~40–45° down, three-quarter, island fills roughly the middle 75% of the width; HUD lives in the empty corners. The level map (04) is the one exception: full-bleed terrain.
- **Hebrew text** came out unusually clean. Known slips: 03 result card says "מכננה" instead of "מכונה"; 01 play-button label is fine ("הפעל זרימה"). Use the game's own strings.
- **RTL slips:** 05 orders the stamps left-to-right (יסוד leftmost); in the game put יסוד rightmost. 01's tray has the magnet card on the left and funnel on the right; RTL would put the first card on the right. The level map (04) does read right-to-left (level 1 bottom-right → 12 top-left).
- **Socket look (01):** turquoise-filled brass disc with a white "+", sitting inline on the pipe right before the fork; it is the brightest thing on screen. In 02 the drum sits on the same brass disc.
- **Flow direction cues (02):** small white chevron arrows inside the glass pipes, pointing right→left. Cheap to reproduce with a scrolling texture.
- **03 has no tray and no play button** (the result card owns the bottom of the screen) and its board is a single straight pipe, not a fork. 03's board is visibly wider than 01/02.
- **05** keeps the level-01 board blurred behind; the magnifier is linked to the source with a dashed line and the mine cart gets a yellow outline glow.
